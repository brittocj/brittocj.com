Green Cloud: Sustainable Computing and Carbon-Aware Architectures

📅 May 7, 2026
⏱️ 3 min read

Building environmentally responsible infrastructure, energy-efficient operations, and sustainability metrics.

Cloud efficiency and environmental responsibility align more than they conflict. Wasted compute burns money and carbon.

Efficiency Levers

Right-size instances. Schedule non-production environments. Use autoscaling. Choose regions with renewable energy where latency allows. Optimize code before adding hardware.

Measurement

Cloud providers publish carbon footprint tools. Tag workloads for accountability. Include sustainability in architecture reviews alongside cost and performance.

Sustainable cloud is efficient cloud operated with intention.
